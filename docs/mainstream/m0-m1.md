# Mainstream pivot — M0 + M1 (2026-10)

## M0: legacy pump.fun archive

* `git mv` (history kept) into `apps/api/app/legacy/pump/`:
  * `providers/` pumpfun_paper, pumpfun_live_paper, pumpfun_decode, pumpfun_curve_math, pump_verify
  * `discovery/`, `traders/`, `wallet/` (Solana), `strategies/pump_paper_v1.py`
  * `marketdata/` pump_search, universe
  * `paper/` launch_tape, launch_study, exit_study, entry_factors, holders, postmortem,
    shadow_compare, executability, manual_trade, board, markets, real_fill, replay
  * `routes/` pumpfun, curve, universe, watch, search, board, markets, trade_ticket, wallet,
    strategy, and `stats_pump.py` (executability / postmortem / exec-report split out of `routes/stats.py`)
* The path is `app/legacy/pump` (inside the `app` package) rather than `apps/api/legacy/pump`
  so imports stay absolute and the module history follows the move. Older docs that cite
  `app/paper/executability.py` etc. now mean `app/legacy/pump/paper/...`.
* Toggle: `AUU_LEGACY_PUMP` (default `off`). `app.main.create_app(legacy=None)` reads it.
  * off: routers health, auth/leaderboard, live, majors, events, stats/paper-performance,
    mainstream, ws. No pump loop, discovery or provider feed is created. A leftover
    `DATA_PROVIDER=pumpfun_*` resolves to the inert mock provider. WS hello reports
    `venue=CEX`; provider channel subscriptions answer `LEGACY_OFF`.
  * on: everything above plus all legacy routers and background tasks (pre-M0 behaviour).
* Kept in place (core): auth (gate, email code, invite, lockout, CSRF, admin-only writes),
  `live/` (gate + LIVE_API_LOCKED), `paper/` ledger, events, decision_log, evidence, books,
  broker, guard, execute, pipeline, legacy_cleanup, `risk/`, `marketdata/` cex/tickers/fetch.
* Health in mainstream mode: `provider=cex_public`, `marketData=real`, `legacyPump=false`,
  `runningStrategies=[]`, `autopaperStall={stalled:false, active:false, reason:no_running_strategy}`.
  In legacy mode `autopaperStall` is also forced to `stalled=false` unless the loop runs
  and autopaper is on.
* Tests: pump-only tests moved to `apps/api/tests/legacy_pump/` (with their fixtures).
  `install_test_isolation()` sets `AUU_LEGACY_PUMP=on` (setdefault) so that suite runs
  unchanged against `app.main.app`, and `AUU_MAINSTREAM_REFRESH=off`.
